FAQ

Before you begin.

Will our data go to external AI providers?

It depends on the models, parsing engines and APIs, not just deployment location. Even an internal server or VPC may make external calls. We review data flows, transfer scope and storage locations during design and agree on processing boundaries.

How does an ontology differ from RAG?

RAG retrieves information to inform a model’s answer. An ontology defines the meaning of business entities, relationships and rules. They can work together, but not every use case needs a complex graph system.

Can you work with scans and CAD drawings?

We process PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets, images and DWG/DXF drawings by type, preserving table and drawing structure. HWP conversion is assessed during discovery. Handwriting and poor scans may reduce accuracy, so each document type is evaluated against reference data first.

Do we need to replace existing systems?

Our starting point is integration with your existing email, drives, collaboration tools and ERP. Available integration methods are assessed during discovery.

What if the pilot does not meet expectations?

We evaluate the pilot against reference data. If it misses the agreed criteria, we report the causes and may recommend not scaling.

How is pricing determined?

We quote after reviewing integrations, data permissions and operating requirements, alongside the proposed pilot scope. Training fees depend on participant numbers and course design.

Who operates the system after delivery?

We provide training and operating manuals for your team. Ongoing support can be arranged under a separate agreement.
