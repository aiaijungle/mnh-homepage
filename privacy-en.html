Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

MNH Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) maintains this Privacy Policy to protect users’ personal information and address related concerns in accordance with South Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act.

1. Information collected and purposes

CategoryInformationPurpose
Consultation requestRequired: company/department, name, email and consultation details. Optional: solutions of interest and data environment.Receive and respond to inquiries, and provide quotations and proposals.

The Company collects only the minimum information needed for consultation. It does not collect confidential documents or sensitive information such as resident registration numbers.

2. Retention and use period

Information is retained for one year after the consultation is completed, then destroyed without delay. If a contract follows, records are retained for the period required by applicable law, such as five years for contract and withdrawal records.

3. Disclosure to third parties

The Company does not disclose personal information to third parties without the user’s consent, except where permitted by law.

4. Outsourced processing

The Company does not currently outsource personal information processing. If this changes, the service provider and processing activities will be disclosed in this policy.

5. Destruction procedures and methods

Information is destroyed without delay when the retention period expires or its purpose has been fulfilled. Electronic files are deleted using methods that prevent recovery, and paper documents are shredded.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, or suspension of its processing at any time. Requests made through the contact method below will be handled without delay.

7. Security measures

  • Restrict access to authorized personnel
  • Encrypt data in transit using HTTPS
  • Maintain access logs and conduct regular checks

8. Cookies and analytics

The Company does not currently use cookies or visitor analytics tools. Any introduction of these tools will be disclosed in this policy.
